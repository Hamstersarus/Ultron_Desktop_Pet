Getting Started

  1. Download and install

    Pick your system in the Download section and open the file.

  2. Get a free OpenRouter key

    Ultron's brain runs on free AI models from OpenRouter. Sign in at openrouter.ai, open Keys → Create Key and copy the key (it starts with sk-or-). In Privacy settings, allow free models.

  3. Give Ultron the key

    On first launch he asks for it. Paste it into the box and click Save. He checks it and remembers it from then on.

  4. Ask him something

    Click 💬, type your question and press Enter. His eyes turn yellow while he thinks, then he answers in his speech bubble. Click the bubble to skip the typing.

  5. Let him roam

    He flies around on his own and rests between trips. Drag him wherever you want. Right-click him to turn flying off or to quit.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free and uses free AI models through your own OpenRouter key.

He says "My mind is overloaded"

The free AI servers are shared by many people and are sometimes busy. Wait a moment and ask again.

He says "My access key is invalid"

Click ⚙️ and paste your key again. Make sure you copied all of it, starting with sk-or-.

Is my key safe?

Your key is stored encrypted on your own computer, using your system's password store. It is only ever sent to OpenRouter.

Are my questions private?

Free AI models may log the questions they receive. Don't ask Ultron anything private.

Does he work offline?

He still flies, blinks and hovers, but he needs the internet to answer questions.
